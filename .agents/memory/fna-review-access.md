---
name: FNA review access
description: Role boundary for viewing submitted Financial Needs Analyses in the OPIAN portal.
---

Clients can open and submit the FNA without signing in. Full saved FNA answers must be viewable by advisors and administrators, but saved records must not be publicly readable.

**Why:** The FNA contains personal and financial information, and the user explicitly requested public client submission plus complete staff review.

**How to apply:** Keep create access public, protect list/detail APIs by advisor or admin role, and show every submitted form field in both staff review screens.
